Top 10 Cat Breeds 🐱

Meet ten of the most popular and beloved cat breeds.

01

Maine Coon

One of the largest domestic cat breeds, Maine Coons are friendly, intelligent and affectionate.

📏 Large ❤️ Friendly 🧶 High Grooming
02

Ragdoll

Ragdolls are famous for their gental personalities, beautiful blue eyes and soft coats.

📏 Large ❤️ Very Friendly 🧶 Medium Grooming
03

Persian

Persian cats are known for their luxurious coats, expressive faces and calm personalities.

📏 Medium ❤️ Calm 🧶 High Grooming
04

Siamese

Siamese cats are social, vocal and highly intelligent companions.

📏 Medium ❤️ Social 🧶 Low Grooming
05

British Shorthair

A calm and easygoing breed with a distinctive round face and plush coat.

📏 Medium-Large ❤️ Easygoing 🧶 Low Grooming
06

Sphynx

The Sphynx is famous for its unusual hairless appearance and outgoing personality.

📏 Medium ❤️ Outgoing 🧼 Special Care
07

Scottish Fold

Scottish Folds are recognizable by their distinctive folded ears and sweet personalities.

📏 Medium ❤️ Sweet 🧶 Medium Grooming
08

Abyssinian

Abyssinians are active, curious and playful cats that love exploring their surroundings.

📏 Medium ❤️ Playful 🧶 Low Grooming
09

Bengal

Bengals have striking spotted coats and energetic, playful personalities.

📏 Medium-Large ❤️ Energetic 🧶 Low Grooming
10

American Shorthair

A friendly and adaptable breed known for its strong build and easygoing nature.

📏 Medium-Large ❤️ Adaptable 🧶 Low Grooming
